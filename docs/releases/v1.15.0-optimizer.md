# v1.15.0 — Strategy optimizer, score v2, sizing v2

Phase 4 of `docs/ROADMAP-v2.md`. Closes A1 (score), A2, A8–A13, A15, A16. The
tuned algorithm is now in place end to end; Phase 5 builds the simulator that
tests it. Method notes: `docs/algorithms/optimizer-v2.md`.

## New

- **17 structures** with delta-grid strike search (`structures.py`): long
  call/put, covered call, cash-secured put, debit and credit verticals, iron
  condor, iron butterfly, long/short straddle, long/short strangle, calendar,
  diagonal, call ratio spread. Exposure signatures replace the v1 lookup table.
- **Score v2** ranks by EV per unit of tail risk (CVaR 5%) after costs, under
  the distribution the user chose; every component is size-invariant.
- **Sizing v2**: 2σ stress loss as the risk unit, buying-power cap, quarter-
  Kelly when a model edge exists, Reg-T naked-margin estimate for undefined
  risk. Covered calls and cash-secured puts no longer size to zero: they show
  one lot and say when it exceeds the risk budget.
- **Cost model**: half-spreads, commissions, expected assignment fee.
- **Market calendar**: NYSE holidays 2024–2028, index close times.
- `POST /detect` accepts `mode` (market | vrp | directional | both),
  `conviction`, `maxBuyingPowerPct`, `kellyFraction`, `stressSigmas`, `costs`,
  `forecastMethod`, `asof` (point-in-time screen). The response carries
  `mode`, `vrp`, `forecast`, `scoreVersion: 2`, and per-candidate
  `evaluation`, `costs`, sizing limits and exposure.

## Changed

- `services/detector.js` is now a thin wrapper around one `screen_v2` engine
  call; `candidates.js` and `strategyMapping.js` are deprecated (kept for v1
  tests only).
- Frontend types accept the new structure names, `mode`, and the v2 score
  breakdown; the Scoring tab's re-weighting applies to v1 breakdowns only
  until Phase 6 teaches the UI the v2 components.

## Known limits

- Calendars/diagonals are valued at the near expiry with sticky-strike vol
  (labelled `approx`).
- The forecast horizon is one number per screen (window midpoint); VRP is
  assumed constant across the term structure.
