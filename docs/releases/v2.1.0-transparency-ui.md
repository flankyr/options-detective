# v2.1.0 — Transparency UI

Phase 6 of `docs/ROADMAP-v2.md`. The engine's v2 numbers reach the screen,
with the reasoning behind each one a click away.

## New

- **Rank by** switch on the Screener: Analyzer (market odds, no edge claimed),
  Vol premium, My view (with a conviction slider), Both. Persisted.
- **Screen context banner**: what the list was ranked on, ATM IV vs forecast
  with its band, the VRP ratio and z-score, which side of vega is eligible,
  the data source and date.
- **Score v2 breakdown** on every card (EV/tail risk, POP, theta per capital,
  liquidity, model confidence) with hover explanations; legacy v1 snapshots
  keep the old re-weightable bar.
- **EV badge** beside POP on every card, under the ranking distribution.
- **Why these numbers** drawer per card: market odds vs your scenario for EV,
  POP, P(max), CVaR, EV per tail risk, Kelly and expected move; cost
  breakdown; which sizing cap bit and the 2σ stress loss; vol source per leg,
  rates, dividend yield, data provider and date, exercise style, exposure.
- **Analyzer**: EV after costs, CVaR, P(max profit) and expected move for the
  position being analysed.
- Glossary: EV, CVaR, VRP, Kelly, SVI smile, early exercise, calibration;
  composite-score and data-freshness entries rewritten for v2. Settings →
  Scoring explains that v2 weights are fixed.
