# v1.13.0 — Pricing core v2

Phase 2 of `docs/ROADMAP-v2.md`. Closes A5, A7 and the greeks half of A4.
Method notes: `docs/algorithms/pricing-core-v2.md`.

## New

- **Black-Scholes-Merton** (`bsm.py`): dividend yield, per-expiry risk-free
  rate from the Treasury curve, vectorised over whole chains. Engine entries
  `bsm_price`, `bsm_greeks`, `bsm_implied_vol`.
- **American exercise** (`american.py`): CRR binomial with tree greeks and the
  early-exercise premium; vectorised Barone-Adesi-Whaley for screening;
  `critical_price` boundary for assignment warnings; European/American style
  table (SPX, XSP, NDX, RUT, VIX are European). Engine entries
  `american_price`, `american_greeks`, `exercise_style`.
- **Our own implied vols on every contract** (`ivModel`), solved from mid, with
  the vendor's number kept beside it and flagged when they disagree by >15%.
- **Volatility smile per expiry** (`smile.py`): SVI fit with the Durrleman
  butterfly check, spline fallback for thin chains, total-variance
  interpolation across expiries. Serialised on the payload as `smiles[exp]`
  and evaluable from Node via `smile_iv`.
- Chain payloads carry `ivSmile` per contract, `atmIvModel`, `atmIv30`,
  `exerciseStyle` and a `volModel` summary (counts of solved / flagged / no-IV).

## Changed

- Detector and Calculator price greeks with `bsm_greeks` (dividend yield and
  the expiry's rate), legs take the smile vol at their strike (`ivSource` says
  which vol won), and the screen's sigma is the smile's ATM vol. Candidate
  `meta` now records `dividendYield`, `provider`, `asof`, `volModel`,
  `exerciseStyle`.
- `POST /calculate` accepts `dividendYield`.

## Tests
